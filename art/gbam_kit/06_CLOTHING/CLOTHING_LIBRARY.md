Design:
- large simple folds
- rounded hems
- no fabric micro-wrinkles
- shell thickness 0.003–0.008 m
- prioritise colour-block readability

Hoodie:
- hood separate or clearly segmented
- cream lining variant useful for the braided male reference

Patterns:
Use large motifs: flowers, stripes, simple geometry.
Avoid brand logos.
