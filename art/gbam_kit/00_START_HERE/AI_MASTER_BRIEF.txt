PROJECT: GBAM CHARACTER SYSTEM v1

GOAL
Build an original family of modular stylised 3D characters in Blender from the supplied in-house references and specifications.

DO NOT
- copy a commercial toy or packaging design
- imitate a named animation studio or living artist
- make the characters photorealistic
- use realistic skin pores or individual hair strands
- make facial anatomy sharp or adult-realistic
- merge all pieces too early
- change the established signature proportions without documenting the change

STYLE
A warm, handcrafted, storybook-toy 3D look:
- large rounded head
- simplified small body
- large expressive eyes
- graphic cheeks
- geometric rounded nose
- friendly mouth
- chunky hairstyles and braids
- matte materials
- soft colour blocking
- subtle texture/grain
- slight handmade asymmetry
- clean readable silhouette

MODELLING
Use subdivision-friendly geometry. Prefer quads for the deforming base mesh. Use separate objects for eyes, irises/pupils, teeth, tongue, hair, headwear, clothing, and major accessories. Keep face modules replaceable.

RIGGING
Create a clean humanoid rig plus facial controls/shape keys. Eyes must rotate independently. Jaw and mouth must animate. Hair/headwear follows the head. Clothing should be easy to swap.

OUTPUT
Produce:
- clean Blender file
- organised collections
- named modular assets
- LOD-ready geometry
- UVs
- simple PBR materials
- neutral A-pose or relaxed T-pose for full body
- front portrait pose for presentation renders
- GLB export test
- preview render matching the camera/lighting specification

Use the dimensions and naming conventions included in this kit.
