# Base Head Specification

Canonical head local bounding box:
- Width: 0.440 m
- Height: 0.500 m
- Depth: 0.400 m
- Origin: centre of head
- Forward: -Y

Shape:
- rounded-square/oval hybrid
- broad forehead
- fullest width through upper cheeks
- soft taper toward chin
- slightly flattened rear cranium compared with a realistic skull
- no realistic facial planes carved in at base stage

Topology:
- target 3,000–6,000 quads before accessories
- clean loops around eyes and mouth
- enough loops for cheek raise, blink, smile and jaw movement
- no unnecessary micro-topology

Keep the nose and graphic cheek discs modular during the first build.
The final production version may blend the nose into the face if deformation tests are better.
