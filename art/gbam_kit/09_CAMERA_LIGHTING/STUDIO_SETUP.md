# Camera & Lighting

## Portrait camera
- lens: 80 mm
- portrait render: 1024 x 1024
- camera approximately eye level
- avoid wide-angle distortion
- head fills roughly 70–80% of frame height

## Full-body camera
- lens: 55–70 mm
- 1024 x 1536 or 1080 x 1920
- neutral standing pose

## Lighting
Three-light soft setup:
1. Key: large area light, front-left and slightly above
2. Fill: weaker large area light, front-right
3. Rim: soft area light behind/above

Suggested relative power:
- key 1.0
- fill 0.35–0.50
- rim 0.25–0.40

Use soft shadows.
World/background: pastel mint, coral, blue, yellow, purple, or warm cream.
