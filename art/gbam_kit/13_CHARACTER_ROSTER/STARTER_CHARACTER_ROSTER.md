# Starter Character Roster

These are suggested original characters to build from the same modular system.

## CHR_M_001_COILS_YELLOW
Young Black boy, short dense coils, yellow hoodie, curious expression.

## CHR_F_001_PUFFS_OVERALLS
Black girl, two large puff buns with small flowers, green overalls, coral undershirt.

## CHR_M_002_BRAIDS_OLIVE
Young Black male, cornrows with hanging braids, olive hoodie with cream lining.

## CHR_F_002_BEADS_PURPLE
Black girl/teen, long box braids with a few large amber beads, purple knit top.

## CHR_F_003_HIJAB_CORAL
Young Muslim woman/girl, coral hijab, cream floral top.

## CHR_F_004_HIJAB_YELLOW
Young Muslim woman/girl, mustard/yellow patterned hijab, warm orange clothing accent.

## CHR_M_003_KUFI_CREAM
Adult Black Muslim man, cream patterned kufi, short beard, cream tunic.

## CHR_M_004_DURAG_NAVY
Young Black man, dark durag, subtle goatee, black/navy hoodie.

## CHR_F_005_HEADWRAP_GREEN
Adult Black woman, bold green headwrap, gold stud earrings, patterned blouse.

## CHR_M_005_FADE_STRIPES
Teen boy, clean short fade, striped sweater, slightly mischievous smile.

## CHR_F_006_LOC_BOB_BLUE
Young woman with chunky shoulder-length locs, blue sweatshirt, calm expression.

## CHR_OLDER_001_GREY_COILS
Older Black adult, short grey coils, soft smile, warm rust cardigan.

Keep all of them recognisably in the same GBAM family.
