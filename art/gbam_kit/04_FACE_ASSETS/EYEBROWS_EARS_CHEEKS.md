# Brows, Ears, Cheeks

Brows:
- 0.085–0.105 m wide
- thick, graphic
- small taper at the outer end
- individual eyebrow hairs are unnecessary

Ears:
- 0.095 x 0.115 x 0.035 m canonical
- simplified C-shaped inner detail
- slight size variation encouraged

Cheeks:
- 0.060–0.078 m
- circular or soft oval
- usually material/decal or ultra-shallow mesh
- warm coral/orange-red family
- signature feature: keep visible in most characters
