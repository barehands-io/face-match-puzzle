# Eye Library

Base:
- diameter: 0.078 m
- slightly flattened sphere/dome
- off-white sclera
- large brown iris
- dark pupil
- one primary circular catchlight

Create variants:
- EYE_01_ROUND
- EYE_02_WIDE
- EYE_03_SOFT_OVAL
- EYE_04_SLEEPY

Animation:
- full eyeball rotates
- eyelid/blink comes from head shape keys or separate lid geometry
- keep iris centred unless expression requires otherwise
