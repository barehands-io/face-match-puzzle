# AI Generation Rules

1. One task per pass.
2. Never let the AI silently change scale.
3. Ask it to report object names and dimensions after creation.
4. Ask for front/side/3-4 screenshots before accepting the asset.
5. Correct silhouette first, detail second.
6. Do not add hair detail until head proportions are approved.
7. Do not rig until topology is approved.
8. Do not optimise until the rig and expressions are approved.
9. Reuse approved modules rather than generating near-duplicates.
10. Save versioned files: `_v001`, `_v002`, etc.
