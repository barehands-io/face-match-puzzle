# Naming Convention

Use uppercase category prefixes.

## Characters
`CHR_<gender-neutral-id>_<number>_<descriptor>`
Example: `CHR_M_003_BRAIDS_OLIVE`

## Meshes
- `MESH_HEAD_BASE`
- `MESH_BODY_BASE`
- `MESH_EYE_L`
- `MESH_EYE_R`
- `MESH_NOSE_01`
- `MESH_MOUTH_SMILE_01`
- `MESH_HAIR_CORNROW_01`
- `MESH_HIJAB_WRAP_01`
- `MESH_TOP_HOODIE_01`

## Materials
- `MAT_SKIN_01`
- `MAT_HAIR_01`
- `MAT_EYE_WHITE`
- `MAT_IRIS_BROWN`
- `MAT_CHEEK_CORAL`
- `MAT_CLOTH_OLIVE`

## Armature/bones
- `RIG_GBAM`
- `DEF_` deformation bones
- `CTRL_` controller bones
- `.L` and `.R` Blender suffixes

## Collections
- `00_REFERENCE`
- `01_BASE`
- `02_FACE`
- `03_HAIR_HEADWEAR`
- `04_CLOTHING`
- `05_ACCESSORIES`
- `06_RIG`
- `07_LIGHTS_CAMERAS`
- `08_EXPORT`
