# Hijab Specification

Hijab must be a separate modular asset.

Construction:
- simplified undercap optional
- outer hijab wraps over crown, around jaw, and down over shoulders
- 3–6 large folds only
- shell thickness: 0.004–0.008 m
- small gap from head to avoid z-fighting

Variants:
- HIJAB_01_SIMPLE_WRAP
- HIJAB_02_SOFT_DRAPE
- HIJAB_03_PATTERNED
- HIJAB_04_SPORTY

Materials can carry floral/geometric patterns.
Do not use patterns that are too tiny to read at gameplay distance.
